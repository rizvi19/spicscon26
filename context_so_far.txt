BASELINE RESULT SUMMARY
WiFi-only degrades strongly under WiFi noise/multipath.
VLP-only is good in clean/WiFi-degraded cases but degrades under optical blockage.
Static fusion often follows VLP because VLP likelihood is sharper.

DSP RESULT SUMMARY
DSP filters reduce signal variance, especially for noisy WiFi.
DSP localization strongly improves WiFi-only error across scenarios.
DSP for VLP/static fusion is scenario-dependent:
- can help VLP-blocked cases
- can hurt clean/high-confidence VLP because smoothing introduces lag

GSP RESULT SUMMARY
GSP sample heatmap is good for Fig. 4:
- smoothing spreads probability mass
- peak probability decreases
- entropy increases
- peak remains near true position

Full-trajectory GSP with argmax produced no metric change because peak cells stayed unchanged.
