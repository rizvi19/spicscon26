==========================================================================================

Working paper direction:
Topology-Aware Adaptive Fusion of WiFi RSSI and Visible-Light Signals for Robust Simulated Indoor Positioning

Safe claim boundary:
This is a simulation-based, physically motivated, reproducible stress-test. The project must not claim real-world deployment, state-of-the-art performance, a new WiFi/VLP propagation model, a new VLP hardware system, or a new RL algorithm.

Dependency order being followed:

- Moving average, median, and EMA reduce signal variance, especially for noisy WiFi scenarios.

- DSP filtering consistently improves WiFi-only localization for the current seed.
